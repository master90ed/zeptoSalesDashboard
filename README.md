🚀 Unlocking Data Insights: Zepto Monthly Sales Performance Dashboard

I recently built an interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Zepto's monthly sales performance to uncover key revenue drivers, regional demand trends, and product category breakdowns.

Key Takeaways from the Analysis:

Total Revenue: Generated over 6M in monthly revenue collection across top product lines.

Top Performing Regions: Hyderabad, Pune, and Bangalore lead total order volumes across major metro cities.

Category Contribution: Snacks and Beverages represent the largest share of overall volume, followed by Dairy and Grocery.

Pricing & Discount Trends: Analyzed original vs. current sales pricing to track revenue variances across regional zones.

Building this helped me sharpen my skills in data visualization, geospatial mapping, and waterfall analysis for quick-commerce metrics.
